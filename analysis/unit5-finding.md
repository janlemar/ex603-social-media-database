## Key Findings

After looking at the results, I noticed two things that might be of concern/attention. 

### 1. Posts with Higher Removal Rates

Post 11 had the highest removal rate at 31.25%, followed by Post 9 at 28.57%. This shows that some posts had more removed likes than others.

I think leadership should take a closer look at these posts to see if there is anything worth investigating. But, the data doesn't tell us exactly why these likes were removedd.

### 2. Posts with No Engagement

Posts 14 and 15 did not receive any likes. I noticed these posts appeared in the LEFT JOIN results but were missing from the INNER JOIN results because they had no matching records in the likes table.

Leadership could look into whether these posts need more visibility or promotion. It would also be worth checking if the posts were published and available.

## Recommendations

- Take a closer look at posts with higher removal rates.
- Investigate as to why Posts 14 and 15 have no recorded likes.
- Maybe find more ways to improve visibility for posts with little or no engagement.
- Continue to monitor these results to see if similar patterns appear in the future.
